The masthead of an article: kicker, an 18ch title, a lead at reading measure, and a row of byline facts.

```jsx
<ArticleHead kicker="Field notes" title="How the ecosystem shares one theme"
             lead="One stylesheet, many subdomains." meta={['@harith', '9 March 2026', '6 min read']} />
```

Facts are divided by 1px hairlines, not bullets or dashes. The head closes with a rule; articles have no cover image.
