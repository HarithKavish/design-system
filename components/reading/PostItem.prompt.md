An index row: title leading, date right-aligned, summary quiet beneath, hairline between entries.

```jsx
<div className="post-list">
  <PostItem title="Bamboo, briefly" date="2026-03-08" summary="A short note." tags={['plants']} href="#" />
</div>
```

Use PostItem for long archives (reads as an index) and BlogCard for a browsable grid of a dozen or fewer.
