A lighter card for explanatory content: a title and a paragraph, no pill, no closing link.

```jsx
<div className="panel-grid">
  <Panel title="Long-term vision" body="Products are expected to evolve over years." />
  <Panel title="No custom forks" body="Shared improvements are preferred over fragmented code paths." />
</div>
```

Use Panel for "why" content and Card for "what you can open" content. `panel--wide` carries long legal or detail prose.
